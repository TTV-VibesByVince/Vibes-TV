# Vibesmas Express

For the holidays, alerts roll across the screen as a train instead of a card.
The locomotive says what happened, and each passenger car carries a person:
their picture in the lit window and their name on a gold plate. When a hype
train starts, the TV can play a video of your own with a glowing gold **Hype
train started!!** title.

It turns itself on between two dates: **November 25 to December 31** to start
with. Settings > **Vibesmas** changes the dates, turns it always on, or off.

## The trains

| Alert | The train |
| --- | --- |
| Follow | NEW FOLLOW, and a car with their name |
| Sub, resub | NEW SUB or RESUB, their car with the months and tier, and their message on a banner behind the caboose. Tier 3 rides first class. |
| Gift subs | The gifter in first class, then one car per person who got a sub, up to 10. Past 10, the caboose says how many more are aboard ("+15 passengers"). |
| Bits | The cheerer's car, then coal cars full of bit gems (more for bigger cheers), and their message on a banner |
| Raid | The raider in first class, cars full of passengers, and the number of raiders on the caboose |

Shoutouts, watch streaks, modiversaries, gigantified emotes and TikTok gifts
keep their usual cards.

A short train rolls in, stops in the middle for a few seconds (with a bell),
and rolls out. A long one, like 10 gift subs, crawls past instead so every
name gets its moment. The track, a snow bank and falling snow appear with the
train and go away after it.

## Settings

Settings > **Vibesmas**:

- **When**: between the dates, always on, or off. Dates are MM-DD
  (`11-25`, `12-31`), and can wrap the new year (`12-15` to `01-05`).
- **Direction**: left to right, or right to left. The lettering always reads
  the right way.
- **On the TV** and **On the stream overlay**: where the train runs. On the
  overlay it runs along the bottom, full width. Turn one off and that screen
  keeps its usual cards.
- **Train sounds**: a whistle, the station bell and chuffing. They're made by
  the browser, so there are no sound files to set up. TV browsers may stay
  quiet until the page has been clicked once.

The **Try it** buttons send test alerts (they use the gift count, bits and
raiders from the Alerts tab's test buttons).

## Hype train video

Pick a video in Settings > Vibesmas > **Hype train video** (or upload one
there; it goes in the media folder on your NAS). When a hype train starts, the
TV plays it full screen with **Hype train started!!** in gold, landing 2.4
seconds in, then goes back to normal. Alerts that come in meanwhile wait until
it's done. The hype train bar on the overlay works as before.

A video made for it works best: about 8 seconds, 16:9, with dark sky in the
top third where the title goes. **Video volume** sets how loud its sound is
(50% to start with).

It plays when the hype train starts, not at each level. Test it with **Start
a hype train** on the Vibesmas tab (then **End the hype train**).
