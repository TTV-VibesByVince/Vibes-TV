Lights

Vibe TV calls these when a card starts and ends. {hex} becomes the colour picked from the raider's avatar. For Govee Lights Controller (V1.3+), turn on Settings > Accept requests from my network on the PC, then use its address, e.g. http://<pc-ip>:8765/color/{hex}.

Theme

Changes the confetti, the floating shapes on the follow screen (hearts, bats, snowflakes, clovers), the raid's warp colours and the theme colour. Clicking a theme switches every TV right away.

Schedule

Between these dates (every year) the scheduled theme is used instead. The first matching row wins; outside them, the theme picked above.

Text & colours

Change the words on each screen. Leave a box empty to use the default (shown in grey). {name}, {months}, {amount} and the like are filled in; text in [square brackets] only shows when its {placeholder} has a value, e.g. [Last seen playing {game}]. The colour is used for the screen's glow and accent, and for your lights.

Backup

All your settings in one file: timings, text, colours, theme and schedule, light URLs and Twitch keys. Logo files aren't included; they're in the media folder.

MixItUp URLs
